# About Me

<img src="/ruc.jpg" class="floatpic" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;" alt="Dinghao Xi at Renmin University of China">

I am Dinghao Xi (奚鼎昊).

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Digital Economics at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Renmin University of China. I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Wei Xu](https://info.ruc.edu.cn/jsky/szdw/ajxjgcx/jjxxglx1/js_5/2d290100ef644d59838c602d85769ae8.htm) and [Prof. Dandan Qiao](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/disa/bio/qiaodd/).

## Research Interests

- Multimodal Learning/Fusion
- Causal Inference
- Social Media Analytics

My current research focuses on practical problems that artificial intelligence faces in real life. My interests are in **multimodal deep learning**, **causal inference**, and their applications to **online video**.
